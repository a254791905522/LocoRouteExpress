Data & privacy

Loco Route Express

A plain-language summary of how this railway puzzle app may handle information on your device and through Apple’s distribution platform.

Product

Loco Route Express (iOS). This page describes the App Store build only—not unrelated websites or services.

Advertising

The app may show ads. Ad networks and mediation partners operate under their own privacy policies when an ad is displayed.

What may be collected

  • Gameplay on your device

    Level progress, settings, and session data are typically stored locally in the app sandbox so you can resume runs without creating an in-game account.

  • Usage and diagnostics

    As disclosed on the App Store, usage-related data may be processed for analytics, advertising, or similar purposes. Categories and partners can change when the app is updated—refer to Apple’s “App Privacy” label for the current listing.

  • Identifiers for ads

    Where ads are shown, device or advertising identifiers may be used to deliver and measure campaigns, subject to your iOS privacy settings and regional rules.

Your choices

Remove local data

Delete the app from your device, or clear its data in iOS Settings, to remove information stored in the app sandbox.

Limit tracking and ads

Use Settings → Privacy & Security (including App Tracking Transparency where available), Screen Time, and content restrictions. Options depend on your iOS version and region.

Apple-managed services

Downloads, updates, billing through Apple ID, and certain device diagnostics are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy—not by how this game stores progress on your device.

This overview is not legal advice. If anything here conflicts with the installed app or the App Store “App Privacy” section, rely on the store listing and on-device behavior. The policy may be updated when features or data practices change; check the App Store label after major updates. No personal contact details or third-party developer homepages are listed on this page.
