Loco Route Express

Free iOS 15+ Puzzle iPhone & iPad

Rebuild the line before the locomotive arrives. Loco Route Express is a strategic railway puzzle: broken tracks stand between the train and its destination, and you have only a limited set of route pieces to lay a safe path in time. Every placement counts—run out of track or misalign a junction, and the run ends in a crash.

How to play

  1. Survey the damage — Study the broken section, available pieces, and where the train is headed.
  2. Lay the track — Connect route segments so the locomotive can reach the destination safely.
  3. Plan ahead — You only get a fixed number of pieces; wasted placements can leave the line incomplete.
  4. Use boosters — Deploy special tools on tough stages to recover from tight spots and finish the run.

Time pressure
